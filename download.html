How to Download & Read

Follow these steps to save the book and start reading.

01

Download the book

Tap the button below. The book will save to your device, usually in your Downloads folder.

Download Book
02

Open the book

Find Beauty-beyond-bruises.html in your Downloads or Files folder. Open it with a browser while connected to the internet.

03

Start reading

Turn pages with the on-screen arrows, or swipe across the book on a touch screen. The page number in the bottom toolbar shows where you are.

Inside the flipbook

Reader buttons at a glance

These icons appear in the floating toolbar along the bottom of the book. Tap or click one to use it.

1 / 54

Left arrow: go back one page. Right arrow: go forward one page. The number between them shows your current page and the total pages.

Music notes: turn the background music on or off.

Headphones: play or pause the page narration. Narration is on when the book opens; tap the icon to pause it or turn it back on.

Bookmark: open the table of contents, then choose a section to jump straight to it.

Keep an internet connection when opening the downloaded file so the flipbook reader can load. You do not need to install an app.
